# CSS Prep Hub

CSS Prep Hub is a one-stop, AI-powered platform designed to assist aspirants in preparing for the Central Superior Services (CSS) exams in Pakistan. It provides a comprehensive suite of tools to cover every stage of the preparation journey, from studying past papers to preparing for the final interview.

## ✨ Key Features

- [x] **Past Paper Viewer**: Browse a vast collection of past papers filterable by subject, year, and category. Includes ideal answers to help with study and revision.
- [x] **Subject-Wise Practice**: Hone your skills with a large bank of practice questions organized by subject, topic, and difficulty level.
- [x] **AI Answer Checker**: Get instant, detailed feedback on your written answers from an AI examiner powered by Google's Gemini. Supports both text and image (OCR) input for handwritten answers.
- [x] **AI Tutor**: Chat with a knowledgeable AI assistant trained on all the past papers. Get instant guidance, explanations, and answers to your CSS-related queries.
- [x] **Interactive Quizzes**: Test your knowledge with subject-specific quizzes, receive immediate scoring, and review your answers.
- [x] **Interview Prep Q&A Bank**: Prepare for the crucial interview stage with an extensive bank of over 600 questions categorized into General Knowledge, Current Affairs, and Personality.
- [x] **Psychological Test Simulation**: Practice for the psychological assessment with simulated personality-based MCQs and sentence completion tests, complete with AI-driven analysis.
- [x] **Progress Tracker**: Stay motivated and track your improvement. The dashboard visualizes your performance over time with dynamic charts and includes a daily streak and "Knowledge Score" system to reward consistent effort.

## 🚀 Future Roadmap

- [ ] **AI Mock Interview**: A fully interactive mock interview experience where users can practice their answers with an AI and receive feedback on their performance and communication skills.
- [ ] **User Accounts & Cloud Sync**: Implement Firebase Authentication for user accounts to save and sync progress, scores, and streaks across multiple devices.
- [ ] **Community Forum**: A dedicated space for aspirants to connect, form study groups, discuss exam strategies, and share resources.
- [ ] **Full AI-Generated Answers**: Expand the AI capabilities to generate full, detailed answers for past paper questions on demand.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js, React, TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS, ShadCN UI
- **AI/Generative**: Google Genkit, Gemini
- **Backend (Future)**: Firebase (Authentication, Firestore)
